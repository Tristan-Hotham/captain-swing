Captain Swing:

did the riots follow the machines?

In 1830 farm labourers across southern and eastern England smashed threshing machines and set ricks alight. Was it the machines, or hunger, wages, enclosure and the poor law? I mapped {{counts.incidents_all|int}} Swing incidents in {{counts.parishes_all|int}} parishes to find out.
